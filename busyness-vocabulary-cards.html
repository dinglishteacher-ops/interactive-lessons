7
Practice · translation

Translate into English

Use the word from the cards. Write first, then open the key.

  1. Зайнятість стала предметом гордості.
    Busy-ness has become a badge of honour.
  2. Нам потрібно, щоб інші підтверджували нашу зайнятість.
    We need other people to validate our busy-ness.
  3. Це як зараза: усі навколо постійно заявляють, що зайняті.
    It's contagious — everyone around you is constantly proclaiming their busy-ness.
  4. Він схопив книжку, коли мчав на літак.
    He grabbed the book while racing to catch a plane.
  5. Я зрозумів це, хай і підсвідомо.
    I realised it, albeit subconsciously.
  6. Якщо ти не зайнятий, тебе вважають ледачим.
    If you're not busy, you are seen as lazy.
  7. Ми настільки звикли до цього, що випускаємо з уваги, наскільки це дивно.
    We're so used to it that we lose sight of how strange it is.
  8. Коли відсутність вільної хвилини стала приводом для хвастощів?
    When did never having a spare moment become something to boast about?
  9. Статус тепер залежить від того, що ми можемо показати людям.
    Status now comes from the things we can show people we're doing.
  10. «Зайнятий» прирівняли до прогресу й успіху.
    'Busy' was equated with progress and success.
  11. Вони одержимі тим, щоб демонструвати свою зайнятість.
    They're obsessed with demonstrating their busy-ness.
  12. Це була цілком завчена відповідь.
    It was a completely pre-programmed response.

Speaking · personalisation

Answer using the target word

The word in bold must appear in your answer, and your answer must be at least two sentences.

  1. What is treated as a badge of honour in your industry? Do you buy into it?
  2. Is there something you're a bit obsessed with at the moment?
  3. When did you last roll up your sleeves and fix something yourself?
  4. What's your pre-programmed response when someone asks how you are? Is it honest?
  5. Who in your life is most likely to boast about how busy they are?
  6. Name a person at work who really delivers. What makes them different?
  7. Is success in Ukraine equated with money, status, or something else?
  8. What do you lose sight of when you get too busy?
  9. When was the last time you were racing to catch something — a plane, a train, a deadline?
  10. What would you put in a post-holiday ritual of your own?
