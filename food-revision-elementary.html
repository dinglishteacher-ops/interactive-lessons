Countable or uncountable?

14 words

Read each word and choose countable or uncountable.

Як це працює Countable — можна порахувати, є множина: an apple → two apples.
Uncountable — не рахуємо штуками, немає множини й немає a/an: milk, bread, rice. Щоб порахувати uncountable, беремо порцію: a glass of milk, a piece of bread, a kilo of rice.

Talk: name three countable and three uncountable things in your fridge right now.

4

like or would like

8 sentences

Choose the correct form for each sentence.

Як це працює like = любити взагалі, завжди: I like fish. Do you like coffee?
would like ('d like) = хотіти зараз, ввічливе прохання: I'd like a coffee, please. Would you like a sandwich?
Після would like ставимо to + дієслово: I'd like to order a pizza. Після like — -ing або to: I like cooking.
