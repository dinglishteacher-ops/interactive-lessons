How to play
  • Take turns choosing a tile. The bigger the number, the harder the task.
  • Answer out loud using inversion. The teacher decides who gets the points.
  • Two tiles are secret Double cards: they're worth twice as much!
  • Chain Story: both players add a sentence in turn. The best story wins the points (or both get them).
  • Use the timer if you want to add pressure. 😉
