Part A

was / were

Was / were — це минулий час дієслова to be (бути): «був, була, було, були».

I / he / she / ityou / we / they
+I was tired.They were at home.
−She wasn't late.We weren't hungry.
?Was it cold?Were you at work?

There was + одна річ, there were + багато: There was a cat. There were three cats. Після everybody / nobody — was.

Питання з питальним словом: Where were you? Why was she late?

З was/were не потрібне did: Did you be at home? → Were you at home?

                  Part D

                  Speak & translate

                  D1

                  Answer the questions out loud. Use full sentences and add one more detail.

                  1. Tell me about the best day of last summer. Where were you? Who were you with? What did you do?
                  2. What was the last book or article you read? What was it about? Did you like it?
                  3. What was the best present you ever got? Who gave it to you and why?
                  4. Did you fly anywhere last year? Where did you go, and what was the most interesting thing there?
                  5. Who taught you to swim, to ride a bike or to cook? Was it easy or difficult?
                  6. What did you wear to the last party or wedding you went to? Why did you choose it?
                  7. Tell me about a time you forgot something important. What happened?
                  8. When did you begin to learn English? What was your first teacher like?
                  9. Did you keep anything from your childhood — toys, letters, photos? Where do you keep it now?
                  10. Tell me about a time somebody didn't understand you. What did you say, and what did they do?

                  D2

                  Translate into English. If your answer is different from the model but means the same, you can mark it as correct.
