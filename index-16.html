Part 1

My last year

Говоримо про власний досвід і висловлюємо думку. Мінімум граматики — максимум розмови.

Нагадування перед розмовою

Маркери часу

last year, in 2025, in summer, in March, three months ago, at the end of the year, when I was 20

ago стоїть після періоду: two weeks ago = два тижні тому.

Типові помилки

  • I didn't went → I didn't go
  • When you went? → When did you go?
  • I was go → I went
  • Last year I have a dog → I had a dog

Як розгорнути відповідь

Факт → причина → деталь → почуття:

I went to Odesa. I went there because my friend got married. We stayed by the sea for a week. It was amazing!

    Part 2

    Stories and questions

    Ставимо питання і будуємо власні історії.

    Питання й зв'язки для історій

    Питання з did

    Wh- + did + subject + V1?

    Where did you go? How long did you stay? How much did it cost?

    Питання до підмета — без did!

    Коли питаємо хто? що? зробив:

    Who called you? What happened?

    Who did call you? What did happen?

    to be

    Were you nervous? How was the trip? Where were you?

    Зв'язки для історії

    First → Then → After that → Suddenly → In the end

    but — але, so — тому, because — тому що, when — коли

    Get a topic. Secretly decide: tell a true story or invent one. Talk for one minute. Your teacher (or partner) asks five questions and then guesses: true or lie? Swap. speaking

    3.2 Hot seat interview

    Get a role card. You are this person and you are on a TV show. Answer the interviewer's questions with lots of details. Then swap roles. role-play

    Click "New role" to start

    Interviewer's questions

    1. When did it happen?
    2. How did it start? Why did you decide to do it?
    3. Who was with you?
    4. What was the best moment?
    5. What was the worst / most difficult moment?
    6. What did your family say?
    7. What did you learn?
    8. Did your life change after that? How?

    3.3 Then and now

    Discuss the questions. Compare the past with today: "When I was a child, I didn't have a phone, but now…" Give an example for every answer. discussion

    1. What did you do in your free time when you were ten? What do children do now?
    2. How did you talk to your friends before smartphones?
    3. What did your family eat on holidays when you were small? Is it the same now?
    4. What was the most popular music or TV show when you were a teenager?
    5. What did you want to be when you were a child? Why didn't / did it happen?
    6. What was better in the past? What is better now?
