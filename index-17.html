Warm-up: Two truths and a lie

all 3 tenses5–7 min

Write three sentences about your life: two are true, one is a lie. Use each pattern once. Your partner (or teacher) asks follow-up questions and guesses the lie.

  • I've never… / I've … twice
  • I've been …-ing for / since…
  • Last year / In 2019 I …

Follow-up questions to ask:

  • When did that happen?
  • Why have you never…?
  • How long have you been…?
  • Who were you with?
  • How did you feel?
Teacher's example

1. I've never eaten sushi. · 2. I've been playing the guitar since I was 12. · 3. Last summer I swam in the Black Sea at 5 a.m.

Головне правило розмови: починаємо з Present Perfect, деталі — у Past Simple.
— Have you ever been to Lviv? — Yes, I have. — When did you go? — I went there last summer.

1.1 Choose the correct form

Choose the correct option. Say why before you click.

1.2 Have you ever…?

Ask the question on the card. If the answer is “Yes, I have”, ask at least three follow-up questions in Past Simple. If it's “No, I haven't”, ask: Would you like to? Why / why not?

  • When was it?
  • Where were you?
  • Who were you with?
  • What happened next?
  • How did you feel?
  • Would you do it again?

1.3 This year so far

Answer in Present Perfect, then add one or two details in Past Simple. Then ask your partner (or teacher) the same question.

    Part 2: Present Perfect Continuous

    PP Continuousvs Present Perfect25 min
    Граматика — коротко

    have / has + been + V-ing

    Коли вживаємоПриклад
    Дія почалась у минулому і триває досі — акцент на тривалостіI've been learning English for five years / since 2021.
    Дія щойно закінчилась, але видно наслідкиYour eyes are red. Have you been crying?
    Те, що відбувається останнім часом (lately, recently)I've been sleeping badly lately.
    PP vs PPC: Present Perfect = результат, скільки зроблено → I've written three emails.
    PP Continuous = процес, як довго → I've been writing emails all morning.
    Увага! Дієслова стану (know, like, love, want, believe, understand, own, have = мати, be) — тільки Present Perfect: I've known her for ten years, не I've been knowing.

    2.1 Simple or continuous?

    Choose the correct option. Watch out for state verbs!

    2.2 Be a detective

    Read the situation and make two or three guesses: What has he / she been doing? Then check the ideas. Bonus: has this ever happened to you? Tell the story in Past Simple.

    2.3 How long…?

    Interview your partner (or teacher). After each answer, ask the Past Simple follow-up. Questions marked ! have a state verb — which tense do you need?

      Part 3: Mixed practice

      PSPPPPC20 min

      3.1 Spot the mistake

      Each sentence has one mistake. Say the correct sentence out loud, then check.

        3.2 Catching up

        Anna and Max meet after five years. Put the verbs in the correct tense. Then read the dialogue in roles and continue it for 2 more minutes.

        3.3 Role-plays

        Choose a situation. Student A starts. In a 1-on-1 lesson, the teacher plays B. Use all three tenses — try to use each one at least twice.

        3.4 Big questions

        Get a random question and talk for at least one minute. Your partner asks one follow-up question.
