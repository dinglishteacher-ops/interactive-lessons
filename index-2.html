Reported speech for pre-intermediate. Seven tasks and four speaking stops, about 45 minutes. Your answers are saved on this device.

  • What questions does a police officer usually ask a witness?

Part 1. The verb steps back

Only the tense changes here. Pronouns, time and place come in Part 2.

Task 1

Read what the person said. Choose the correct verb form for the reported sentence.

Task 2

Type the verb in the correct tense. Press Enter to check. You have two tries.

Speaking stop: You said…

Say three sentences about yourself. Your partner or teacher reports each one back to you: “You said you…”. Then swap.

  • I usually … in the evening.
  • Right now I'm …
  • Last weekend I …

Part 2. Who, when and where

The verb still steps back. Now watch the pronouns and the time and place words.

Task 3

Choose the correct time or place expression.

Task 4

Who is speaking, and to whom? Choose the correct pronouns.

Speaking stop: This week's news

Think about people you talked to this week. Report three things they said. Use a time or place expression in each sentence.

  • My friend said …
  • Someone in my family said …
  • My boss / teacher / neighbour said …

Part 3. Everything together

Tense, pronouns, time and place in one sentence.

Task 5

Rewrite the sentence in reported speech. The beginning is done for you. You have two tries.

Task 6

Each reported sentence has one mistake. Tap the wrong word.

Speaking stop: The robbery

A jewellery shop was robbed ten minutes ago. One of you is a witness: choose a card and read it aloud. The other is a reporter: listen, then report to your editor: “She said she…”. Swap roles with a new card. Working one-to-one? Your teacher is the first witness.

Iryna, shop assistant

“I work here every day.”

“Two men came in a few minutes ago.”

“My hands are still shaking now.”

Bohdan, taxi driver

“I'm waiting for a passenger.”

“I saw a black car here yesterday.”

“I don't know the driver.”

Mrs Hall, neighbour

“I live above the shop.”

“I heard a loud noise tonight.”

“My dog is barking at the door.”

Extra: invent a fourth witness. Say three more sentences for your partner to report.
