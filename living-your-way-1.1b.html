1
Grammar · the big picture

One idea, three structures

Read the summary before you start. The difference is who does the work.

Active

I repaired my laptop.

Я сам це зробив. Ми знаємо виконавця і він важливий.

Passive

My laptop was repaired.

Важливо, що сталося з предметом. Хто зробив — невідомо, очевидно або неважливо.

Have sth done

I had my laptop repaired.

Я організував, щоб це зробив хтось інший (майстер, салон, сервіс) — або зі мною сталося щось погане.

Reference · form
  • Passive = be (у потрібному часі) + V3. Змінюється тільки be: is made / was made / has been made / will be made / must be made / is being made.
  • Have sth done = have (у потрібному часі) + об'єкт + V3. Порядок слів жорсткий: had my hair cut, не had cut my hair.
  • Розмовний варіант: get sth done — I got my car serviced. Значення те саме, стиль менш формальний.
  • Питання та заперечення будуємо через допоміжне: Where did you have your hair cut? — I haven't had it cut yet.
  • Без by, якщо виконавець очевидний: She had her nails done (зрозуміло, що в салоні).

Grammar · form drill

Change the tense, keep the verb

The verb is always send. Only the form of be changes.

  1. Present simple: The invoices every Monday.
  2. Past simple: The invoice yesterday.
  3. Present continuous: The invoice right now.
  4. Present perfect: The invoice already.
  5. Past perfect: The invoice before I checked.
  6. Future: The invoice tomorrow.
  7. Modal: The invoice by Friday.
  8. Past continuous: While the invoice , the system crashed.

Reading · lifestyles

Three people, three lifestyles

Read the three texts. Which lifestyle does each person have: sedentary, active or nomadic?

A
Jake

I take after my parents — none of us can stand being indoors. Our farm has been handed down from generation to generation, so there is always something waiting to be done. I took over the running of it when I was twenty, and these days I'm out there at least ten hours a day. I do look forward to retiring at some point, but that won't happen for a good few years yet.

B
Suki

I took over the family law firm ten years ago. When my mother handed over the responsibility to me, I had no idea how much work it would be. I spend most of my day at a desk trying to keep up with our cases — twelve hours sitting down and roughly six cups of coffee. I'm looking into hiring another lawyer to ease some of the pressure.

C
Meredith

I used to work in the city, but in the end I couldn't put up with the noise and the crowds. I kept dreaming about escaping, and eventually I couldn't put the decision off any longer. I saved up, quit my job and bought a camper van. Now I travel all the time and make a living doing odd jobs. I don't think anything should keep you from chasing your dreams — I'm living mine.

Speaking: which lifestyle would you prefer, and which one is closest to yours right now?

Vocabulary · multi-word verbs

Match the verb to the definition

All ten verbs are in the texts above. Find them first, then choose.

  1. start being responsible for a job that someone else was doing before you
  2. be similar to your father or mother because you have a similar character or appearance
  3. give control or responsibility for something, e.g. a company, to someone
  4. give something to a younger person so that they continue a tradition
  5. delay doing something until later
  6. accept an unpleasant situation without complaining
  7. find out about something so that you can take action
  8. be excited about something that is going to happen
  9. prevent someone from doing something
  10. understand all the most recent facts about something
Словничок
  • take after sb — бути схожим на когось із батьків /teɪk ˈɑːftə/
  • take over (sth) — перебрати на себе, очолити (справу, посаду)
  • hand over (sth) to sb — передати комусь контроль чи відповідальність
  • hand sth down — передати у спадок, з покоління в покоління
  • put sth off — відкладати на потім /pʊt ˈɒf/
  • put up with sth — терпіти, миритися з чимось неприємним
  • look into sth — розібратися, вивчити питання (щоб діяти)
  • look forward to sth/doing — з нетерпінням чекати
  • keep sb from doing sth — заважати комусь, не давати щось робити
  • keep up with sth/sb — бути в курсі; встигати за кимось

Vocabulary · how they work

Two words or three?

Read the two rules and complete the examples.

Rules
  • Multi-word verbs are idiomatic — значення зазвичай не вгадаєш зі складових слів. Put up with не має нічого спільного ні з «класти», ні з «угору».
  • A multi-word verb has two or three words: перше слово — дієслово, друге (і третє) — прийменник або частка.
  1. verb + preposition, two words — example: take
  2. a multi-word verb with three words — example: look
  3. another three-word verb from the list — put
  4. and one more — keep
