To be: am, is, are

Elementary. Present Simple of the verb to be in all sentence types, question words, and he / she / his / her.

Part 1. Am, is, are

Дієслово to be у Present Simple

Українською ми часто пропускаємо «є»: Я студент. Англійською дієслово обов'язкове: I am a student.

СтверджувальнеЗаперечнеПитання
II am (I'm)I am not (I'm not)Am I…?
he / she / itshe is (she's)she is not (she isn't)Is she…?
we / you / theythey are (they're)they are not (they aren't)Are they…?

Питання: am/is/are стає на перше місце: She is at home → Is she at home?

Короткі відповіді: Yes, I am. / No, I'm not. Yes, he is. / No, he isn't. Yes, they are. / No, they aren't.

У короткій відповіді «так» скорочення не буває: Yes, I am ✓   Yes, I'm ✗

Speaking

  • Say 3 true and 2 false sentences about yourself: I'm 25. I'm not a doctor… Your teacher guesses which are false.
  • Ask your teacher 4 questions with Are you…? / Is your…? and answer with short answers.

Speaking

  • Interview your teacher about a friend or a family member: What's his name? How old is he? Where is he from? Why is he your best friend?

Part 3. He, she, his, her

Хто? і Чий?

Хто? (перед дієсловом)Чий? (перед іменником)
чоловікhe — він: He is a pilot.his — його: his car
жінкаshe — вона: She is a nurse.her — її: her bag

Не плутайте: he's = he is (він є), his = його. He's my brother. His name is Max.

Speaking

  • Describe a man and a woman from your family: name, age, job, city. Use he / his and she / her.
